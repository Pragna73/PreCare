🚀 Excited to share my latest end-to-end healthcare engineering project: PreCare — AI-Powered Pregnancy Monitoring & Clinical Risk Analysis Platform!

Maternal and fetal health during pregnancy requires continuous, proactive tracking. However, complex lab reports and delayed risk identification often cause anxiety and critical care delays. 

To bridge this gap, I designed and engineered PreCare — a unified digital healthcare ecosystem consolidating a native iOS mobile application, a clinical web portal, a high-throughput Python FastAPI backend, and automated multi-layer testing pipelines.

---

💡 What PreCare Does:
• 📄 Clinical Medical Report Analysis: Extracts maternal biomarkers (Hemoglobin, Blood Pressure, Glucose, FHR) from lab reports and classifies maternal risk (Good, Warning, Danger) with actionable clinical next steps.
• 💬 Maya AI Maternal Assistant: An intelligent pregnancy triage assistant offering trimester-specific guidance (exercise safety in Week 32/34, dietary guidelines, kick counting, and emergency triage).
• 🩺 Digital Twin & Health Vitals: Real-time monitoring of maternal vitals, kick counters, and prenatal doctor appointment scheduling.
• 🚨 One-Tap Emergency Triage: Instant emergency contact notification and nearby maternity hospital routing.

---

🧠 Architecture & Technology Stack:
• 📱 Mobile: Native SwiftUI (iOS 17+), MVVM Architecture, Xcode, Codemagic CI/CD.
• 🌐 Web Platform: React 18, TypeScript, Vite, TailwindCSS, Supabase integration.
• ⚡ Backend API: Python FastAPI (40 REST endpoints), SQLAlchemy, SQLite with Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) for zero-lock concurrency.
• 🤖 AI & OCR: Google Gemini Flash LLM orchestration, intelligent fallback rules, and automated medical report OCR extraction.

---

🧪 Rigorous Automated Testing & Quality Engineering:
Quality and reliability are non-negotiable in digital health. PreCare features a comprehensive automated testing suite:
• 🌐 300 Automated Selenium Tests: Validating clinical test cases across the web portal with automated HTML test reporting.
• 📱 305 Automated Appium Mobile Tests: Testing iOS authentication, interactive password visibility toggles, Maya AI chat, and appointment booking flows.
• ⚡ Load & Performance Benchmarks: Stress-tested with Locust (50 concurrent users) achieving 836+ requests/sec with a 3.94 ms average response time.
• 🛡️ DevSecOps & SAST: Integrated `pip-audit` for dependency CVE scanning, Bandit for static code analysis, and Flake8 linting.

---

⚙️ Unified CI/CD Automation (GitHub Actions):
Configured 6 automated workflows ensuring continuous quality on every push:
1. `combined-tests.yml` — Master end-to-end validation pipeline
2. `selenium-tests.yml` — Automated Selenium web test suite
3. `appium-tests.yml` — Automated Appium mobile test suite
4. `backend-security.yml` — Dependency vulnerability & SAST scans
5. `load-tests.yml` — Headless Locust concurrency benchmarks
6. `package-release.yml` — Automated packaging of the complete project into a single clean release ZIP (`PreCare-complete-project.zip`)
